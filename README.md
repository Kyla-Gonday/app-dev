# My Favorite Movie: Captain America – Civil War

![Captain America: Civil War Poster](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/5/53/Captain_America_Civil_War_poster.jpg)

##  Overview
**Captain America: Civil War** is a 2016 superhero film produced by **Marvel Studios** and distributed by **Walt Disney Studios Motion Pictures**.  
It’s the **third Captain America movie** and part of the **Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU)**.  

The film shows what happens when **the Avengers are divided** after a government decision to control their actions.

---

##  Main Conflict
> *“United we stand, divided we fall.”*  

When an international incident leads to **collateral damage**, the government introduces the **Sokovia Accords**, a law to regulate superheroes.  
This causes a huge disagreement between:
- **Team Iron Man (Tony Stark)** – Supports government control  
- **Team Captain America (Steve Rogers)** – Fights for independence  

---

##  Main Characters
- **Captain America (Chris Evans)**   
- **Iron Man (Robert Downey Jr.)**   
- **Black Widow (Scarlett Johansson)**   
- **Black Panther (Chadwick Boseman)**

##  Why I Love It
I love this movie because it combines:
1. Intense action scenes   
2. Emotional storytelling   
3. Amazing teamwork (and team breakups )  
4. Introduces new heroes like **Black Panther** and **Spider-Man**
